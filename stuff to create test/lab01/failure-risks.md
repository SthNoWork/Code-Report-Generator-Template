# Failure risks · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 10/21/2026)

| #  |                     How the project could fail               | Chapter 01 topic | Early warning sign | First countermeasure | Owner |
|----|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|--------------------|----------------------|-------|
| F1 | Inaccurate or failed attendance logging due to session/login timeouts and authentication bugs during peak event check-in times. | Problem Statement | High rate of user complaints about login failures and manual attendance override requests after club meetings. | Implement robust session management, local error caching, and fallback offline check-in options. | Lead Developer |
| F2 | Low adoption rate among students and club leaders who continue using legacy group chats and paper sheets. | Today's Workaround | Fewer than 20% of active campus clubs creating accounts or uploading course/event materials within the first month. | Conduct hands-on onboarding sessions with club leaders and introduce single-tap QR check-in to prove convenience over paper. | Project Manager |
| F3 | Ambiguous positioning leading users and evaluators to view the system as a generic file portal rather than an integrated hub. | Product Vision | Stakeholders ask how the platform differs from standard cloud drives or existing social channels. | Refine and communicate the unique value proposition uniting materials, directory, and automated attendance. | Product Owner |
| F4 | Scope creep caused by attempting to build full backend systems in C++ while designing complex web/mobile UIs within a single semester. | Scope Definition | Team missing early milestones or spending disproportionate time debugging C++ server bindings instead of core logic. | Strictly enforce the defined Scope boundaries (C++ core backend/session logic only, web/mobile frontend UI modelled only). | Technical Lead |
| F5 | Resistance or lack of support from lecturers and advisors due to privacy concerns or misalignment with university policies. | Stakeholder Management | Slow response times from faculty advisors when requested to review or pilot the platform. | Schedule early administrative briefing demos highlighting automated reporting and data security controls. | Stakeholder Liaison |
