# Problem statement · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 10/21/2026)

## Problem

The problem of logging in affects students' experience in using ITC Club Hub;
the impact of which is inaccurate self-record attendance.
A successful solution would record students' attendances more accurately.

## Vision

For lecturers, students, or parents who do not know about us, ITC Club Hub is a central platform, directory, or student union portal that manages and coordinates various student-led clubs, activities, and extracurricular initiatives.
Unlike a normal school app, our product provides many services for users such as containing the materials for students including slides, labs, quizzes, and many more.

## Today's workaround

Students register through chat groups, while organizers manually maintain participant lists in spreadsheets or messages.

## Scope

- Built in C++ this semester:
  * Core backend service logic or modular performance components (if required by course specifications).
  * Secure credential hashing and token-based session management routines.
- Modelled only (web and mobile):
  * Interactive UI/UX wireframes for student dashboards, club directory pages, and mobile attendance check-in interfaces.
  * Responsive screen designs for the course material repository (slides, labs, and quizzes).
- Out of scope:
  * Automated facial recognition or biometric attendance verification.
  * Deep integration with external university registrar or official ERP/SIS databases.
  * Native mobile app store deployment (restricting mobile access to responsive web interfaces).
